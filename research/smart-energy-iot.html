An AI-Based Occupancy-Aware Smart Energy Management System for Classroom Automation Using Computer Vision and IOT

PEER-REVIEWED JOURNAL ISSN: 2319-8478 VOL. 15 | ISSUE 2 PUBLISHED: 23 APR 2026
Authors: Teena Yadav (Assistant Professor), Anjali Dewda, Aryan Ahirwar • SAGE University, Indore
Journal: Vindhyan Bloom Research Journal (IT & Business Analytics Section)
Manuscript ID: VB-2026-0013 • Indexed on Zenodo & Google Scholar
Persistent DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19737528 ↗

Abstract

Educational campuses routinely experience avoidable electricity waste when lighting, fans, and HVAC appliances remain energized across unoccupied or partially occupied classrooms. This research proposes and evaluates an occupancy-aware smart energy management system combining real-time edge computer vision with IoT microcontroller relay actuation. By continuously estimating human presence and zone density locally rather than relying solely on brittle motion timers, the system orchestrates classroom electrical loads dynamically—significantly lowering energy consumption while preserving student comfort and operational reliability.

Architecture Highlights

01. Edge Computer Vision

Real-time occupancy estimation from classroom feeds detects stationary and active occupants, overcoming passive infrared (PIR) sensor blind spots.

02. IoT Relay Automation

Microcontroller-driven relay modules translate zone-level occupancy decisions into safe, automated switching for lighting and ventilation circuits.

03. Sustainable Campus Impact

Reduces idle power drain between lecture periods and scales affordably across Indian university classrooms using commodity edge hardware.
