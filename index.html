[ 02_PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH ]

Published Research Paper

Peer-reviewed academic research integrating artificial intelligence, real-time computer vision, and IoT automation for intelligent occupancy-aware energy management.

PEER-REVIEWED JOURNAL ISSN: 2319-8478 VOL. 15 | ISSUE 2 PUBLISHED: 23 APR 2026

An AI-Based Occupancy-Aware Smart Energy Management System for Classroom Automation Using Computer Vision and IOT

Authors: Teena Yadav (Assistant Professor), Anjali Dewda, Aryan Ahirwar • SAGE University, Indore
Published in: Vindhyan Bloom Research Journal (IT & Business Analytics Section)
Manuscript Ref: VB-2026-0013 • Indexed in Zenodo / Google Scholar
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19737528 ↗

This paper designs and evaluates an occupancy-aware automated energy management architecture. Utilizing edge computer vision detection paired with IoT microcontroller relays, the system dynamically monitors classroom density and coordinates illumination and environmental controls in real-time, drastically reducing educational campus power waste while maintaining human-centric comfort.
